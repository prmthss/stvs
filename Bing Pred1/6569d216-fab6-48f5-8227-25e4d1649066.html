The Tale of Three Fish Once upon a time, three unique fish lived peacefully in a quiet pond. One evening, a group of local fishermen passed by and noticed the abundance of life beneath the water. “This pond is completely full of fish!” they exclaimed to one another. “We have never cast our nets here before. We must return tomorrow morning with our equipment and catch them all!” When the eldest of the three fish overheard their conversation, deep anxiety struck him. He immediately evacuate this pond at once. They will return tomorrow at dawn, and staying here means certain doom!” The second fish analyzed the situation and quickly agreed. “You make a very logical point,” he said. “It is far better to be safe than sorry. We must leave.” However, the youngest fish simply laughed, dismissing their fears. “There is absolutely no reason to worry,” he scoffed arrogantly. “We have lived in this habitat all our lives, and no human has ever disturbed us. Why keep me safe.” Taking no chances, the eldest fish migrated through a small channel that very night, safely leading his entire family away. Early the next morning, the second fish spotted the fishermen approaching in the distance. Recognizing the immediate danger, he also fled the pond with his family without hesitation. Meanwhile, the third fish stubbornly remained behind, relying entirely on blind luck. Unfortunately, his fortune ran out. The fishermen cast their nets, capturing every remaining creature, including the foolish young fish.
