His planet was scarcely larger than a house, containing three small volcanoes and a single, dramatic rose. The Prince loved his rose deeply, yet her vanity and constant demands eventually broke his heart. Feeling lonely and misunderstood, he decided to leave his home behind, using a flock of wild birds to escape and explore the vast universe. During his celestial journey, the Little Prince visited several neighboring asteroids, each inhabited by bizarre adults. He met a king who claimed to rule over everything, a conceited man who craved only admiration, and a businessman who spent his days greedily counting stars. To the Prince, these grown-ups seemed incredibly strange and narrow-minded, as they only cared about numbers, power, and prestige, completely forgetting what truly mattered in life. Eventually, the Prince landed on Earth, arriving in the middle of a barren desert. It was here that he met a wise cat, who shared a profound secret with him. “It is only with the heart that one can see rightly,” the cat whispered gently. “What is essential is invisible to the eye.” Through this meaningful friendship, the Prince finally understood that his rose was unique and precious simply because of the time and love he had invested in caring for her. With a heart full of clarity and longing, the Little Prince realized it was time to return to Asteroid B-612 to protect his beloved flower.
