The Peacock and His Feathers Long ago, the Peacock did not have the beautiful feathers that make him famous today. His feathers were plain, and he looked much like many other birds. Because of this, he often wished that he could be more attractive and different from the rest. One day, the Peacock went to Juno, the goddess who liked him very much. He asked her for a special gift. “Please give me something that will make me stand out from all the other birds,” he said. Juno listened to his request and decided to help him. She gave him a magnificent train of feathers covered with shining colors. The Peacock was delighted. His new feathers sparkled with shades of green, gold, blue, and purple. Whenever sunlight touched them, they seemed to glow. Proud of his appearance, he walked around showing off his beautiful tail. The other birds stopped to admire him. Some were amazed by his beauty, while others felt jealous. Even the graceful pheasant had to admit that the Peacock was now the most beautiful bird in the area. For a while, the Peacock was very happy. Then one morning, he looked up and saw an Eagle flying high above the clouds. The Eagle moved freely through the sky and seemed strong and powerful. The Peacock suddenly wished that he could fly just as well. He spread his wings and tried to rise into the air. However, his long and heavy tail made flying difficult. No matter how hard he tried, he could not fly high like the Eagle. At that moment, the Peacock realized that every gift comes with a price, and that beauty cannot replace strength and freedom.
