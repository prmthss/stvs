Why We Should Avoid Using Public Wi-Fi Networks Although it is convenient and free to use, public Wi-Fi can expose users to serious security risks. Therefore, people should be careful and avoid using it for sensitive online activities. Unsafe user behavior is a major reason why public Wi-Fi is dangerous. According to the All About Cookies diagram, many users log in to personal accounts or connect to unverified networks, allowing cybercriminals to easily access data unnoticed. Additionally, users often perform sensitive activities like accessing financial accounts or sending private emails. Some even leave devices unattended or accept unknown connections, increasing unauthorized access risks. Thus, public Wi-Fi risks stem from both the network itself and careless user habits. Furthermore, attackers can easily take advantage of these situations by creating fake hotspots that look like real networks. Once users connect, their personal information can be monitored or stolen. In conclusion, while practical, public Wi-Fi promotes unsafe behavior and heightens security risks. Based on the diagram, avoiding public Wi-Fi or switching to mobile data is a wiser choice to protect personal information.
