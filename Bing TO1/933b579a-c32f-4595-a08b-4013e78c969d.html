The Day I Survived a 24-Hour Digital Detox Last weekend, I decided to challenge myself to a 24-hour digital detox. As a high school student, I realized that I spent way too much time scrolling through social media and checking my phone. So, on Saturday morning at 8 AM, I turned off my smartphone and put it deep inside my desk drawer. In the first few hours, I felt extremely anxious. My hand kept reaching for my pocket automatically, and I felt like I was missing out on important chats with my friends. The silence in my room felt strange without notifications. However, by lunchtime, things began to change. Since I could not use my phone, I opened a novel that I bought months ago but never read. To my surprise, I actually enjoyed it and read three chapters without stopping. In the afternoon, I helped my mother bake a chocolate cake and spent hours talking and laughing with her in the kitchen. In the evening, my brother and I dusted off an old board game. We played together for hours, which was something we had not done since junior high school. When I finally turned my phone back on Sunday morning, I had over a hundred unread messages. Surprisingly, I did not care much about them. Surviving a day without my gadget taught me that life outside the screen is much more peaceful and fun. Now, I try to limit my screen time every day.
