A layer of activated charcoal should be put over the pebbles to eliminate odors. The layer of small pebbles at the bottom prevents water from rotting the plant roots. We should place the finished terrarium in an area where it can receive strong, direct sunlight. We must use a long spoon to compress the soil heavily right before creating the planting holes. The depth of the potting soil layer to be added on top of the charcoal should be no more than 5 cm.
